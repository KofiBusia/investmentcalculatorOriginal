How to Use This Suite
Click to expand / collapse the guide
Step 1 — Pick a module
Use the 8 tabs above to switch between valuation models: Equity, Corporate Finance, Derivatives, Fixed Income, Pension/ALM, Private Capital, Real Assets, and Financial Institutions.
Step 2 — Enter your inputs
Type values into the blue-bordered fields. All inputs are in the same units shown in the label (e.g., "000" means thousands, "%" means you enter the number — e.g., type 15 for 15%). Results update instantly.
Step 3 — Read results
Green boxes = positive signals. Red boxes = caution. Amber = neutral. The verdict banner at the bottom of each calculator gives a plain-English decision.
CFA Methodology
Every formula follows CFA Institute standards: FCFF DCF, DDM, RIM, BSM, Newton–Raphson YTM, hazard-rate CDS, LBO waterfall, VC method, and ALM BPV hedging — all from the official CFA curriculum.
Key conventions
• Rates: enter as numbers (e.g., 15 = 15%)
• Monetary amounts: enter in units shown (000 = thousands)
• Negative values: type the minus sign (e.g., −100 for a rate shock)
Ghana Context
Default values are calibrated to Ghana Stock Exchange (GSE) listed companies: GHS discount rates, GoG bond yields, and typical West African risk premia. Adjust to your specific security.
